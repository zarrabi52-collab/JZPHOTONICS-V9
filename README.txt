JZ Photonics Website Version 9 - Netlify Form Ready

How to make email delivery work:
1. Go to Netlify and create a free account.
2. Drag and drop the entire unzipped website folder into Netlify.
3. Submit one test form on the live Netlify site.
4. Open Netlify dashboard > your site > Forms. Confirm "consultation-request" appears.
5. Go to Site configuration > Forms > Form notifications.
6. Add an email notification to zarrabi52@gmail.com.
7. Test the form again.

Important: GitHub Pages alone cannot email form submissions.
